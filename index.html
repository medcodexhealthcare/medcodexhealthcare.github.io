About MEDCODEX HEALTHCARE

We are an India based organisation working with international clients to deliver reliable, secure and high quality healthcare outsourcing, medical data and infrastructure support services.

Who We Are

MEDCODEX HEALTHCARE is an India based healthcare outsourcing and medical data services organisation. We support hospitals, diagnostic centres, radiology groups, healthcare BPO companies, medical AI startups, insurance companies, clinical research organisations and healthcare technology providers worldwide.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, scalable and cost effective healthcare outsourcing, medical imaging data processing, medical AI support and healthcare back office solutions to international healthcare organisations, while creating employment and education opportunities in India.

Our Vision

To become a trusted global partner for healthcare outsourcing and social development, where every hospital gets quality support, every young person gets skills and employment, and every underserved community gets access to education and healthcare.
